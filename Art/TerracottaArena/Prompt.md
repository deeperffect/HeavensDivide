# Terracotta ground generation

Built-in image_gen tool; user screenshot used as the style reference.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production game terrain base-color texture for Unreal, not a mockup. Image 1 is a style and palette reference only. Generate a square, high-resolution, seamless tile of the warm terracotta arena FLOOR in the reference: broad confident hand-painted strokes, warm dusty coral-orange and peach ochre rock/earth, larger flattened angular plates with sparse dark mauve cracks, subtly lit broad facets, scattered small desaturated purple slate chips occupying only about 8% of the tile. Match the reference's polished stylized fantasy arena brushwork and simple chunky forms. Viewed perfectly orthographically from directly overhead, uniform texel density. Edge-to-edge continuous ground; seamless wrapping on all four sides, no perimeter border, no perspective, no scene vignette or central spotlight. The painting itself should have broad tonal variation, not noise. No characters, minions, creatures, props, grass, UI, text, numerals, icons, counters, glyphs, logos or watermarks. The open warm ground is the dominant surface and leaves room for gameplay. This texture will tile beneath separately placed purple stone slabs and a stone rim. Output the texture image only.
