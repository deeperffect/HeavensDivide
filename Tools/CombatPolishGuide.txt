Combat polish

Magnet: break the cyan urn, then collect the floating horseshoe. It pulls all
existing loose XP into the active character, including airborne chest rewards.
It follows character swaps and preserves each drop's XP value. Health pickups
and unopened chests remain available. New drops after activation behave normally.

Balance window (Tools > Bloodshift Balance):
- BP_EncounterDirector: first magnet at 75 seconds; later attempts 100-140
  seconds apart. At most one magnet urn or loose magnet at a time.
- BP_MagnetPickup: collection radius.
- BP_MagnetUrn: reward class. Healing and magnet urns always break on the first
  damaging player hit, regardless of their inherited health settings.
- BP_ExperiencePickup: Magnet Travel Seconds (default 1.8).

For species size, open the enemy Blueprint and select CollisionCylinder >
Relative Scale 3D. The size pass scales body and collision together.

Chest: the three reels show three portions of the actual XP reward, not bonus
items. Space, Enter, gamepad A or click jumps to the reveal. The celebration
pauses gameplay and releases the same XP shower after closing.
